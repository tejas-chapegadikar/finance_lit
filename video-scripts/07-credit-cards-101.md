# Credit Cards 101 — Video Script

**Target length:** ~90 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** A credit card statement highlighting "Minimum Amount Due" in a big tempting button.
**Narration:** "This button is designed to look like the easy option. It's actually the most expensive one on the page."

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:22)
**Visual:** A calendar counting 20 to 50 days, statement balance fully paid off, "0% interest" stamp.
**Narration:** "Pay your FULL statement balance by the due date, and you pay zero interest — that's the grace period. That's the entire point of a credit card, used right."

## Scene 3 (0:22–0:40)
**Visual:** ₹20,000 balance, ₹1,000 minimum paid, remaining ₹19,000 glowing red and growing.
**Narration:** "But pay only the minimum, and the rest starts accruing interest immediately — typically 36 to 48 percent a year. And your grace period disappears entirely, even on new purchases."

## Scene 4 (0:40–0:55)
**Visual:** Ankit character paying ₹1,000 of a ₹20,000 bill, the remaining debt visibly snowballing month over month.
**Narration:** "Ankit pays just the minimum on a twenty-thousand-rupee bill. That remaining balance grows faster than almost any investment could offset. This is compound interest — working against him."

## Scene 5 (0:55–1:10)
**Visual:** A checklist: "Spend only what you can pay in cash," "Pay the FULL balance," "Set up autopay."
**Narration:** "Use it well: never spend more than you could pay in cash, always clear the full balance, and set up autopay so you never forget."

## Scene 6 (1:10–1:20)
**Visual:** An ATM with a credit card, red "no grace period" warning stamp.
**Narration:** "One more trap — cash withdrawals on a credit card have NO grace period and an extra fee. Avoid it completely."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:20–1:27)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "Ready to spot the trap yourself? Take the quiz."

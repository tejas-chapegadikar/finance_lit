# UPI & Digital Payments Safety — Video Script

**Target length:** ~90 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** A phone screen showing a UPI "Collect Request" notification that says "Refund ₹500 — Tap to receive."
**Narration:** "This message looks like it's about to GIVE you money. It's actually about to take it."

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:25)
**Visual:** Split screen — "Scan QR / Enter PIN" arrow pointing OUT of a wallet; "Receive money" arrow needing nothing at all.
**Narration:** "Here's the one rule that stops almost every UPI scam: scanning a QR code or entering your PIN only ever SENDS money. You never need either one to receive money. Ever."

## Scene 3 (0:25–0:40)
**Visual:** A fake "cashback QR" being scanned, money flying OUT of the phone instead of in.
**Narration:** "So when a message says 'scan this QR to get your cashback' — that's not a reward. That's the scam."

## Scene 4 (0:40–0:55)
**Visual:** A scammer character on a phone call, a screen-share icon appearing ominously.
**Narration:** "Same with fake customer care calls asking you to install a screen-sharing app, or share an OTP 'to verify your account.' No real bank agent will ever ask for that."

## Scene 5 (0:55–1:10)
**Visual:** A small real payment arriving, followed by an urgent "please send it back, it was a mistake!" message.
**Narration:** "Watch for the 'wrong transfer' scam too — a small real payment shows up, followed by pressure to 'refund' a bigger amount. That original transfer is often reversed or from a stolen account."

## Scene 6 (1:10–1:22)
**Visual:** A big lock icon over "PIN" and "OTP" text.
**Narration:** "Two things you never share with anyone — your UPI PIN, and any OTP. Not on a call, not in a message, not ever."

## Scene 7 (1:22–1:32)
**Visual:** The number "1930" appearing boldly, alongside a phone and a shield icon.
**Narration:** "If it happens anyway, report it immediately — call 1930, India's cyber helpline. Fast reporting is how transfers actually get reversed."

## Scene 8 — CTA (1:32–1:38)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "Think you'd spot the scam? Prove it — take the quiz."

# Investing Basics: Stocks vs. Mutual Funds vs. FDs — Video Script

**Target length:** ~90 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** A slider moving from "Safe, low return" to "Risky, high return."
**Narration:** "Every investment lives somewhere on this line. There's no single 'best' one — only what's right for YOUR goal."

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:20)
**Visual:** A locked vault labeled "FD — 6-7.5% — guaranteed."
**Narration:** "Fixed Deposits sit at the safe end — a guaranteed return, principal protected. Good for money you can't afford to lose."

## Scene 3 (0:20–0:35)
**Visual:** A single company building, its stock price chart swinging wildly up and down.
**Narration:** "Stocks mean owning a real slice of one company. If it grows, you win big. If it doesn't... you could lose big. High risk, high potential reward, and it takes real research to pick well."

## Scene 4 (0:35–0:52)
**Visual:** One coin splitting into dozens of tiny slices, each landing on a different company logo.
**Narration:** "Mutual funds pool your money with thousands of other investors and spread it across many companies at once. One company doing badly won't sink the whole thing — that's diversification."

## Scene 5 (0:52–1:05)
**Visual:** "Index Fund" label with a chart quietly tracking the Nifty 50 line, low-fee tag attached.
**Narration:** "Index funds are a simple type of mutual fund that just tracks the market instead of a manager picking stocks — low fees, and historically hard for most active managers to beat."

## Scene 6 (1:05–1:18)
**Visual:** Timeline: "<1 year → FD," "1-3 years → Debt fund," "5+ years → Equity/Index fund."
**Narration:** "Match your money to your timeline — safety for short-term goals, equity for the long game where you can ride out the ups and downs."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:18–1:25)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "Stocks, funds, or FDs — think you've got it? Take the quiz."

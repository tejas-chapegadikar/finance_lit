# Common Financial Scams in India — Video Script

**Target length:** ~95 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** A phone ringing urgently, "YOUR ACCOUNT WILL BE BLOCKED IN 10 MINUTES" flashing on screen.
**Narration:** "Urgency and secrecy. If a message has both, it's almost always a scam — no matter how official it looks."

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:25)
**Visual:** A "0% documents, instant loan!" app icon, followed by a hand secretly granting it access to contacts and photos.
**Narration:** "Fake loan apps promise instant cash with no paperwork — then charge hidden fees and threaten to contact everyone in your phone if you're late. Only ever borrow from RBI-registered lenders, and never grant a loan app access to your contacts."

## Scene 3 (0:25–0:40)
**Visual:** A pyramid of investors, money flowing from new joiners at the bottom up to early investors at the top, then the pyramid collapsing.
**Narration:** "Ponzi schemes promise 'guaranteed' returns of 20, 30, even 50 percent a month. They pay old investors using new investors' money — and they always collapse eventually. If a return sounds too good to be true next to a normal 6 to 14 percent, it is."

## Scene 4 (0:40–0:55)
**Visual:** A fake trading app showing fabricated soaring "profits," then a locked withdrawal button.
**Narration:** "Fake crypto and trading 'gurus' show off fabricated profit screenshots to pull you into depositing more — until withdrawals mysteriously stop working."

## Scene 5 (0:55–1:12)
**Visual:** A caller impersonating a bank, pressuring a screen-share install, an OTP request flashing red.
**Narration:** "Phishing calls impersonate your bank, the police, even the income tax department — creating panic to get your OTP, PIN, or remote access to your phone. No real bank or government office works this way over a cold call."

## Scene 6 (1:12–1:25)
**Visual:** A hand picking up the phone to independently call the bank's OFFICIAL number instead of the one just given.
**Narration:** "The single best defense: pause, and verify independently — call the organization's official number yourself, never the one the caller gives you."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:25–1:32)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "Think you can spot every scam in this lesson? Prove it — take the quiz."

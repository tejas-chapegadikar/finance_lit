# Budgeting Basics — Video Script

**Target length:** ~80 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:08)
**Visual:** A student scrolling through a bank app, confused, empty wallet icon.
**Narration:** "Ever check your account a week before month-end and think... where did it all go?"

## Scene 2 (0:08–0:20)
**Visual:** Split screen: money coming in on one side, flowing out in random directions on the other.
**Narration:** "A budget isn't about restricting yourself. It's about deciding in advance where your money goes — instead of wondering where it went."

## Scene 3 (0:20–0:38)
**Visual:** A pie chart splitting into three labeled slices: 50% Needs, 30% Wants, 20% Savings, in violet/orange/emerald.
**Narration:** "Try the 50-30-20 rule. Fifty percent of your money goes to needs — mess fees, transport, recharge. Thirty percent to wants — food, OTT, shopping. Twenty percent to savings and goals."

## Scene 4 (0:38–0:52)
**Visual:** ₹8,000 splitting into ₹4,000 / ₹2,400 / ₹1,600 with animated numbers.
**Narration:** "Say you get eight thousand rupees a month. That's four thousand for needs, twenty-four hundred for wants, and sixteen hundred straight into savings."

## Scene 5 (0:52–1:05)
**Visual:** A hand moving a coin into a "savings" jar the moment money arrives, before it touches a wallet.
**Narration:** "The trick? Pay yourself first. Move your savings out the moment money lands — not whatever's left over, which is usually nothing."

## Scene 6 (1:05–1:18)
**Visual:** Five small ₹100 UPI payment notifications stacking up into one ₹500 total.
**Narration:** "And watch the small stuff — five quiet hundred-rupee UPI payments add up to the same five hundred rupees as one big purchase you'd have thought twice about."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:18–1:25)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge animating in.
**Narration:** "Now go prove it — take the quiz and earn your first seventy-five XP."

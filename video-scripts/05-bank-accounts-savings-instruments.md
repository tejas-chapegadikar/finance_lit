# Bank Accounts & Savings Instruments — Video Script

**Target length:** ~85 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** A student getting hit with a surprise "₹400 minimum balance fee" notification.
**Narration:** "Ever been charged a fee just for not having ENOUGH money in your account? That's the minimum balance trap — and most students don't even know they're in it."

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:25)
**Visual:** Two account cards side by side — "Savings" (person icon, small interest coin) and "Current" (storefront icon, unlimited transactions).
**Narration:** "A savings account is for individuals — it pays a little interest and is meant for keeping money. A current account is for businesses — no interest, but unlimited transactions. As a student, always ask for a zero-balance student account."

## Scene 3 (0:25–0:40)
**Visual:** A lump sum of money locking into a vault labeled "FD — 1 year — 7%," then unlocking with extra coins added.
**Narration:** "A Fixed Deposit means locking a lump sum for a set term at a higher, guaranteed interest rate. Break it early, and you lose a bit of that interest — but your money stays safe."

## Scene 4 (0:40–0:52)
**Visual:** Small monthly coin deposits stacking into the same kind of vault over time.
**Narration:** "A Recurring Deposit is the same idea, but for people without a lump sum — you deposit a fixed amount every month instead, perfect for pocket money or a stipend."

## Scene 5 (0:52–1:05)
**Visual:** Three labeled buckets: "Emergency fund → Savings account," "Saving for a laptop → RD," "Got a scholarship payout → FD."
**Narration:** "Match the tool to the goal — everyday money in a savings account, a monthly goal in an RD, a lump sum you won't touch for a year in an FD."

## Scene 6 (1:05–1:15)
**Visual:** An FD/RD bar barely edging past a rising "inflation" line.
**Narration:** "Just don't mistake these for long-term wealth building — their returns barely beat inflation. They're for safety, not growth."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:15–1:22)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "Know your FDs from your RDs? Take the quiz and find out."

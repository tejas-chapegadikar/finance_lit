# Income Tax Basics for First Salary — Video Script

**Target length:** ~90 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** An offer letter showing "CTC: ₹10,00,000" and a confused student dividing it by 12.
**Narration:** "Ten lakh CTC sounds like eighty-three thousand a month. Your bank account is about to disagree."

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:25)
**Visual:** CTC bar splitting into base salary, employer PF, insurance, and "your actual take-home" shrinking underneath.
**Narration:** "CTC — Cost to Company — includes stuff you never see in your account: employer PF contributions, insurance premiums, and more. Your real take-home is smaller. Sometimes a lot smaller."

## Scene 3 (0:25–0:42)
**Visual:** Two paths forking: "Old Regime — higher slabs, deductions allowed" vs "New Regime — lower slabs, few deductions."
**Narration:** "Every year, you choose between two tax regimes. Old regime: higher tax rates, but you can claim deductions like 80C investments and HRA. New regime: lower rates, but almost no deductions."

## Scene 4 (0:42–0:55)
**Visual:** A "first job, no investments yet" character picking the simpler path.
**Narration:** "With your first job and few investments yet, the new regime is often simpler — and cheaper. Once you start investing seriously, it's worth recalculating."

## Scene 5 (0:55–1:08)
**Visual:** A payslip with "TDS deducted" highlighted, flowing into a "Form 16" document at year-end.
**Narration:** "Your employer deducts tax every month — that's TDS — and gives you Form 16 at year-end, summarizing it all. You use that to file your Income Tax Return."

## Scene 6 (1:08–1:20)
**Visual:** Two job offers with identical CTC numbers, but very different "take-home" amounts revealed underneath.
**Narration:** "Here's the mistake to avoid: comparing job offers by CTC alone. Same CTC, very different take-home, depending on how the salary is structured."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:20–1:27)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "Know your CTC from your take-home now? Prove it in the quiz."

# Understanding Credit Score (CIBIL) — Video Script

**Target length:** ~85 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** A dial/gauge from 300 to 900, needle sitting in the green zone above 750.
**Narration:** "This three-digit number can decide whether you get a loan, a credit card, or even an apartment — years before you think it matters."

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:22)
**Visual:** "CIBIL Score: 300–900" label, with a highlighted "750+" zone marked "Good."
**Narration:** "It's your CIBIL score — a number that summarizes how reliably you repay borrowed money. Above 750 is generally considered good."

## Scene 3 (0:22–0:35)
**Visual:** A parent's loan document with a student's name linked as co-applicant, a small red flag appearing on a missed payment.
**Narration:** "You can have a credit history before you ever own a card — being a co-applicant on a parent's loan, or an add-on on their credit card, links you to their track record. Good or bad."

## Scene 4 (0:35–0:52)
**Visual:** A pie chart: 35% Payment History (largest slice, glowing), 30% Credit Utilization, rest split among other factors.
**Narration:** "The biggest factor, by far, is payment history — did you pay on time? Second is credit utilization — how much of your available credit you're actually using. Even if you pay it off monthly, maxing out your limit looks risky."

## Scene 5 (0:52–1:05)
**Visual:** A single missed payment icon casting a long shadow over several years on a timeline.
**Narration:** "One missed payment isn't just a late fee — it can sit on your report and drag your score down for years, following you into every future loan application."

## Scene 6 (1:05–1:15)
**Visual:** A clean, high score unlocking a "lower interest rate" tag on a loan offer.
**Narration:** "Build a clean history early, and future-you gets better loan terms — for free."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:15–1:22)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "Test what you just learned — take the quiz."

# Compound Interest — Video Script

**Target length:** ~85 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** A single seed growing into a small plant, then a tree, sped up.
**Narration:** "This is the closest thing to magic in personal finance. It's called compound interest."

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:25)
**Visual:** Two piles of coins — one growing in a straight line, one growing in a curve that shoots upward.
**Narration:** "Simple interest pays you on your original amount, every year. Compound interest pays you on your original amount PLUS everything you've already earned. Your money starts earning money on its own money."

## Scene 3 (0:25–0:38)
**Visual:** The formula A = P(1+r/n)^(nt) appearing cleanly on screen, then simplifying to "just let it sit and grow."
**Narration:** "There's a formula for it, but here's the shortcut you actually need: the Rule of 72. Divide 72 by your interest rate to see how many years it takes your money to double."

## Scene 4 (0:38–0:50)
**Visual:** "72 ÷ 8 = 9 years" with a coin pile doubling.
**Narration:** "At 8 percent interest, that's just nine years to double your money. No extra effort. Just time."

## Scene 5 (0:50–1:10)
**Visual:** Two characters, Ravi (age 20) and Meena (age 30), both investing ₹2,000/month, with a graph showing Ravi's bar ending dramatically taller by age 60.
**Narration:** "Ravi starts investing two thousand rupees a month at twenty. Meena starts the exact same amount... at thirty. Same monthly amount, same age of stopping — sixty. But Ravi ends up with far more. Not just a bit more — dramatically more."

## Scene 6 (1:10–1:20)
**Visual:** Text on screen: "Time in the market > timing the market."
**Narration:** "Those extra ten early years are worth more than any ten years added at the end. Time in the market beats trying to time the market."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:20–1:28)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "The best time to start was years ago. The second best time is right now — take the quiz and lock it in."

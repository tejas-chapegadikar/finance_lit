# Saving vs. Spending Psychology — Video Script

**Target length:** ~80 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** A ₹40 cup of chai, looking totally harmless.
**Narration:** "Forty rupees for chai. No big deal, right?"

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:22)
**Visual:** A calendar flipping through a year while ₹40 coins drop in daily, stacking into a large pile labeled ₹14,600.
**Narration:** "Do that every day for a year, and it's fourteen thousand six hundred rupees. Big purchases get scrutiny. Small ones don't — and that's exactly the problem."

## Scene 3 (0:22–0:36)
**Visual:** A salary/allowance bar growing, and a spending bar growing right alongside it at the same pace.
**Narration:** "This is lifestyle inflation — the moment your income goes up, your spending quietly goes up with it, and your savings never actually grow."

## Scene 4 (0:36–0:50)
**Visual:** Two identical ₹2,000 notes — one labeled "birthday gift" being spent instantly, one labeled "part-time job" going into a savings jar.
**Narration:** "Here's a weird one: people treat money differently depending on where it came from. Gift money feels free to blow. Earned money feels worth saving. But two thousand rupees is two thousand rupees, no matter the source."

## Scene 5 (0:50–1:05)
**Visual:** An auto-transfer animation — money arriving and instantly splitting off into a locked savings account, hands-free.
**Narration:** "The fix isn't willpower — willpower runs out. It's automation. Set up an auto-transfer the day money arrives, so saving isn't a decision you make every month, it's one you already made."

## Scene 6 (1:05–1:15)
**Visual:** A finger hovering over "Buy Now," a 24-hour countdown clock appearing, then the finger pulling away.
**Narration:** "And for anything non-essential over a set amount — wait 24 hours. Most impulse buys lose their pull once the moment passes."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:15–1:22)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "Put it to the test — take the quiz and earn your XP."

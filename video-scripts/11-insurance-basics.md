# Insurance Basics — Video Script

**Target length:** ~85 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** A small monthly premium coin dropping in, a huge "protection shield" appearing over a family.
**Narration:** "Insurance isn't an investment. It's the cheapest protection you'll ever buy against the worst-case scenario."

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:25)
**Visual:** A term life policy paying a large lump sum ONLY if something happens during the term — no payout if the term just ends peacefully.
**Narration:** "Term insurance pays a large sum to your family if you die during the policy term. No payout if you simply outlive it — that's exactly why it's so cheap. A healthy 22-year-old can get one crore of coverage for just a few thousand rupees a year."

## Scene 3 (0:25–0:38)
**Visual:** A hospital bill wiping out a savings jar, then a health insurance card stepping in to cover it.
**Narration:** "Health insurance covers hospitalization — the kind of bill that can wipe out years of savings in one incident. Many students are covered under a parent's policy, but check what it actually includes."

## Scene 4 (0:38–0:55)
**Visual:** A "2-in-1 combo product" (ULIP) shown doing both jobs weakly — a small shield AND small growth arrow, both underwhelming.
**Narration:** "Watch out for 'investment-cum-insurance' combo products like ULIPs. They tend to do both jobs poorly — weaker coverage than term insurance, and weaker growth than a plain mutual fund, for a similar premium."

## Scene 5 (0:55–1:08)
**Visual:** Two separate, strong paths: "Term insurance → protection" and "Mutual fund SIP → growth," side by side.
**Narration:** "The better move: buy term insurance for protection, and invest separately through mutual funds for growth. Keeping them separate almost always costs less and does more."

## Scene 6 (1:08–1:15)
**Visual:** A student with no dependents shown as "insurance optional," a parent with a family shown as "insurance essential."
**Narration:** "You mainly need life insurance once someone depends on your income — a parent, a spouse, a sibling."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:15–1:22)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "Term vs. ULIP — know the difference? Take the quiz and find out."

# SIP & the Power of Starting Early — Video Script

**Target length:** ~90 seconds

## Scene 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)
**Visual:** A phone screen with an auto-debit toggle switching ON, ₹500 flowing out monthly on its own.
**Narration:** "What if growing your money took zero extra effort after one single setup? That's a SIP."

## Scene 2 (0:10–0:22)
**Visual:** "SIP = Systematic Investment Plan" with a fixed amount dropping into a mutual fund every month automatically.
**Narration:** "A Systematic Investment Plan means investing a fixed amount into a mutual fund on a fixed schedule — automatically. No trying to time the market. Just showing up, every month."

## Scene 3 (0:22–0:35)
**Visual:** A market price line going up and down, with the same ₹2,000 buying more units when the line dips, fewer when it peaks.
**Narration:** "When prices dip, your fixed amount buys more units. When prices rise, it buys fewer. Over time, this averages out your cost — no stress about buying at the 'right' moment."

## Scene 4 (0:35–0:55)
**Visual:** Two characters, Aisha (21) and Rohan (31), both investing ₹3,000/month until 60 — a growth chart showing Aisha's bar ending dramatically higher.
**Narration:** "Aisha starts a three-thousand-rupee SIP at twenty-one. Rohan starts the exact same SIP at thirty-one. Both stop at sixty. Same monthly amount — but Aisha ends up with dramatically more, because her early years of growth kept compounding on themselves for decades."

## Scene 5 (0:55–1:08)
**Visual:** "Minimum SIP: ₹500/month" tag, a student's small allowance flowing into it.
**Narration:** "You can start with as little as five hundred rupees a month. The habit matters more than the amount, especially this early."

## Scene 6 (1:08–1:18)
**Visual:** A phone auto-debit icon linked to a bank account, running quietly in the background.
**Narration:** "Set it up once through auto-debit, and it runs itself — you don't have to remember a thing."

## Scene 7 — CTA (1:18–1:25)
**Visual:** StockStreak logo, "+75 XP" badge.
**Narration:** "The best time to start a SIP was years ago. The second best time? Right after this quiz."

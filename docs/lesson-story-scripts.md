# Lesson story scripts (for review)

Every lesson below gets the same scroll-story treatment as Budgeting: Riya and Mom, 9–10 scenes, captions of 12 words or fewer, the lesson's own facts and numbers, a recap, then the existing quiz (unchanged).

Nothing here is animated yet. Review the captions and scenes, mark changes, and each approved script becomes one config file in `src/content/stories/`. Lessons stay on the old text page until their story is built.

**Two kinds of comparison.** Some lessons compare two people. To keep the cast to Riya and Mom, the other person is either **Late Riya** (Riya in a grey hoodie, who starts later) or **Mom**.

**New drawings.** Each lesson lists any art it needs beyond what exists today: Riya, Mom, wallet, notes, coins, samosas, jars, lid, lock, calendar, phone, small icons, counters, labels and recap cards.
